# Our arrangement for running the platform

**Between:** Ankit Mani Pandey (Ankit) and Vivek Dubey, for [legal name of Vivek's business] (Vivek)
**Date:** [DD Month YYYY]  **Length:** 12 months, until [DD Month YYYY]

This note is written so that both of us know exactly how this works. It is a formal note of what we have agreed. The signed paper agreement will be made separately and will follow these points. Blanks in [square brackets] are for us to fill in.

---

## 1. What this is
- Ankit owns a software platform for restaurants and shops (customer app, shop owner panel, rider app, admin app).
- Ankit does not yet have his own registered business, GST number or payment-gateway account.
- For 12 months, Vivek's business will be the **operator**: its name and GST number appear on the invoices, and its payment-gateway account receives the shops' payments.
- Vivek and Ankit share the money as described below. At the end, Ankit moves the platform to his own business.

## 2. Who owns what
1. Ankit owns the platform: the app, the source code, the servers, the database, the storage, the backups, the domain and the brand name. All of these stay in Ankit's accounts.
2. Vivek has the right to act as the operator for these 12 months only. He does not get the source code or ownership of anything.
3. The platform's name and web address are [platform name] and [domain]. Ankit can change them.

## 3. What Vivek does
1. Keeps his GST registration, business registration and payment-gateway account valid.
2. Lets the platform issue invoices in his business name and collect shops' payments into his account.
3. **Pays the GST and files the GST returns** on every invoice issued in his name. This is his responsibility.
4. Registers the SMS (DLT) account in his business name and pays its registration fee. The sender name is [sender name].
5. Does not change the keys, fees, invoice settings or any other money setting in the platform. Only Ankit does that.
6. Shows Ankit his payment-gateway settlement statement once a month, so the numbers can be checked.
7. Does not keep back or divert any money that belongs to Ankit.

## 4. What Ankit does
1. Builds, hosts, maintains and supports the platform, including servers, database and backups.
2. Keeps the **Common Sheet** (section 7) correct and open for Vivek to see at all times.
3. Keeps the shops' data safe and uses it only to run the platform.

## 5. How the money is shared
The share is worked out on the amount **before GST**. GST is not income for either of us.

| Who brought the shop | Ankit | Vivek |
|---|---|---|
| Vivek brought the shop | 50% | 50% |
| Ankit brought the shop | 70% | 30% |

- This is the same for the monthly rent, the one-time setup fee, and the SMS balance the shop buys.
- Who brought the shop is recorded in the platform when the shop is created. That record is final unless both of us agree to change it.
- A payment counts on the day the gateway captures it, even if it reaches the bank a few days later.
- If a payment is refunded or cancelled, both shares for it are reversed. A fee that is waived or reduced is shared only on what is actually received. Ankit approves every waiver.
- Payment-gateway and bank charges: [Vivek bears them / shared in the same ratio / taken off the amount before sharing].

## 6. Shared costs: always 50 / 50
These costs are split equally, whoever brought the shop:
- The SMS provider recharge (the money that keeps SMS working).
- Server, database, storage and backup charges.
- API and software-service charges.
- Other running costs we both agree on in writing (for example the domain).

One of us pays the whole cost and gets half back from the other later. Every cost is entered on the Common Sheet with the date, who paid and the bill. A single cost above Rs. [amount] needs the other's approval first, except the SMS recharge. DLT registration fees and our own taxes are not shared costs.

## 7. The Common Sheet
- The platform keeps a sheet that adds a row **by itself** every time a shop pays. Each row shows the shop, what for, the amount before GST, who brought the shop, each person's share, how much Vivek must pay Ankit, the due date and whether it is Pending, Partly paid or Paid. Shared costs are added as rows too.
- The sheet only holds numbers. The real money moves between us outside the platform.
- **Only Ankit can change the sheet.** Vivek can see it and download it at any time, and can add an "I have paid" note with the date and reference on any row. Ankit confirms it.
- Every change is saved with the date, the time and who made it. Nothing is deleted. A mistake is fixed by a new, visible entry.
- If Vivek thinks a row is wrong, he tells Ankit within [15] days. After that the row is taken as accepted.

## 8. When Vivek pays Ankit
1. Vivek pays Ankit his share of each payment **within 15 days** of the day it counts. After 15 days it shows as **late** on the sheet.
2. Payment is by bank transfer or UPI to the account Ankit gives. Vivek writes the sheet reference in the note.
3. **Set-off:** what one owes the other can be set against what the other owes. Ankit records it on the sheet with the amount and a reason.
   Example: Vivek pays a Rs. 5,000 recharge, so Ankit owes him Rs. 2,500. Vivek receives Rs. 4,000 from a shop Ankit brought, so he owes Ankit 70%, which is Rs. 2,800. After set-off Vivek owes Ankit Rs. 300.
4. If a shop pays rent in cash or by bank transfer, the platform records **who received it** (Ankit or Vivek). If Ankit receives it, he settles Vivek's share with him in the same way.
5. If a payment is more than [30] days late, Ankit can end the arrangement early and move the platform (section 10).

## 9. Taxes
- Vivek pays and files the GST on invoices in his name.
- Each of us pays income tax on his own earnings.
- We will each ask our own tax adviser whether TDS applies to the payments between us, and whether Ankit needs to give Vivek an invoice for his share, and we will do what they advise.

## 10. At the end of the 12 months
1. Ankit moves the platform to his own business by changing the business details, the gateway keys and the invoice series in the admin app.
2. From that day the shops pay Ankit's business and **Vivek has no share** of those payments, unless we sign an extension.
3. Invoices issued before that day stay in Vivek's name. If a shop pays one of them later, it is shared as in section 5.
4. Within [15] days we close the sheet and both confirm the final statement by email. Whatever is owed is paid within [30] days after that.
5. The unused SMS balances that shops still have are honoured by Ankit's business. Vivek pays Ankit the part of those balances that has not yet been shared.
6. Vivek helps with the move (notices to shops, closing his gateway links to the platform, statements).
7. After the move, Vivek may still call these shops his past clients, but not say he currently serves them.
8. Extending beyond 12 months needs a written agreement signed by both of us. Nothing is extended automatically.

## 11. Data
All data (shops, customers, riders, orders, payments) stays on Ankit's servers. Vivek may see it only through the admin app, only to run the service. He does not copy, sell or share it, during or after. If either of us finds a leak or misuse, we tell the other within 48 hours.

## 12. Not taking the shops away
During the 12 months and for [12] months after, neither of us will encourage a shop to leave the platform for a competing product.

## 13. Ending early
- Either of us can end it with [30] days' notice.
- Ankit can end it at once and move the platform if Vivek is more than [30] days late with a payment, changes money settings without permission, loses his GST or gateway account, or breaks sections 11 or 12.
- Vivek can end it if Ankit has not paid a shared cost for more than [30] days after a written reminder.
- When it ends early, section 10 applies from that day. Money already earned stays due.

## 14. Keeping it private
We keep each other's business details, this note and the sheet private for [3] years after the end.

## 15. Each of us is separate
We are two separate businesses. This is not a partnership, and neither of us can bind the other to a contract or a debt. Each of us is responsible for his own taxes and actions.

## 16. If we disagree
We first talk to each other and try to settle it within [15] days. If we cannot, we will settle it as the signed paper agreement says.

## 17. Changing this note
Any change must be in writing and confirmed by both of us (an email from each is enough).
