# MOU addendum: plans, prepaid wallet and other client charges (DRAFT)

Draft written 2026-11-09 for Ankit to paste into the MOU or attach as an addendum. It is NOT legal advice: have the CA or a lawyer read it before signing. Wording uses "Agency" (Vivyaan Business Systems) and "Developer" (Ankit Mani Pandey), as in the MOU.

## 1. What counts as Gross Revenue

Gross Revenue from a client shop means all money the Agency receives from that shop for the platform, namely:

1. Plan payments (Growth, Pro) for each 30-day period.
2. Order fees of the Partner plan, taken from the shop's prepaid wallet (Rs 3 to Rs 15 per completed order).
3. Any extra charge the Agency raises for the platform (for example Play Store app setup, menu digitisation), if the Agency and Developer agree it belongs here. Charges for work done only by the Agency's own staff may be excluded if both agree in writing.

## 2. Prepaid wallet (Partner plan)

1. A wallet top-up is an advance. It is counted as revenue only when it is used, that is, when an order fee is deducted. Unused balance is a liability of the Agency, not profit.
2. The wallet can only be spent on platform charges. It cannot be withdrawn in cash or transferred.
3. When a shop moves to a paid plan, any unused wallet balance is credited against that plan's price. That credit is not new revenue; the plan price is counted only for the amount actually paid.
4. If a shop leaves while a balance remains, the Agency decides, in line with its terms with the shop, whether to refund or keep it. Money refunded is not counted as revenue.

## 3. Net Profit

Net Profit is Gross Revenue (as above) less GST, other taxes, payment-gateway fees (Razorpay) and the shared costs already listed in the MOU (servers, SMS, maps and similar). Plan credits, wallet credits and refunds reduce Gross Revenue.

## 4. Plan changes and free periods

1. The Agency may set the plan, price, free days or free period of any shop. Only the Developer's supreme admin login can change these in the platform; the Agency can view them.
2. A free period is not revenue, and no split applies to it.
3. Prices and fee slabs may change from time to time. A change applies to payments made after it.

## 5. Records

The platform's Shop money page and the Razorpay dashboard are the shared record. Each month the Agency shares the totals for plan payments, wallet top-ups, wallet deductions, credits and refunds, so both sides can agree the Net Profit figure.
